Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

Privacy Policy Terms & Conditions

FitCo (“FitCo,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a men’s pants fit recommendation platform. We help users discover pants that are likely to fit well based on the information they provide. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

This policy applies to fitco.fit and covers our quiz, recommendations, and emails.

FitCo doesn’t require you to create an account. If you don’t voluntarily provide identifying information (like your email), we generally can’t identify you personally from your quiz responses alone.

A quick note on what FitCo is not: we’re not an online store, a marketplace, or a manufacturer. We don’t process payments, we don’t sell products, and we don’t have accounts or logins. Recommendations link out to third-party retailers using ordinary links — we currently earn no commission on them.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

The fitting asks six questions. That is the entire list of what you can tell us:

  • What you’re shopping for (jeans, chinos, technical, or no preference)
  • Which of four broad builds best describes you
  • Where pants usually fit wrong for you (up to two options)
  • Your height, as one of four broad ranges — not an exact figure
  • Which leg shape you prefer
  • What matters most to you in a pair of pants

We do not ask for your weight, your waist size, or your inseam, and there is nowhere on the site to enter them. We don’t ask for your name, your address, your phone number, or your date of birth, and we never handle payment details.

Your answers are saved in your own browser (see “Where your answers live” below). You don’t have to answer anything; if you skip a question we simply have less to base a recommendation on.

Separately, you may give us your email address if you choose to join the updates list.

Information we collect automatically

With analytics switched off, we collect nothing automatically. FitCo sets no cookies, loads no third-party scripts, and makes no requests to anyone else when you visit. Our fonts are served from our own domain rather than a font network, so no third party is told that you visited.

If you agree to analytics (see below), our analytics provider would then receive:

  • Which pages and steps you viewed, and which options you chose
  • Your browser and device type
  • Your IP address, from which an approximate city or region is derived
  • An identifier stored in a cookie and in your browser’s local storage, so repeat visits can be recognised

Your fitting answers are included in that analytics data as the shape of the answer (for example, “athletic build”), so we can tell which recommendations the engine is producing. Your email address is never sent to analytics.

2. How we use your information

We use the information above to:

  • Generate and improve your pants fit recommendations
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the website and quiz
  • Understand which pages and features are used (analytics)
  • Respond to your emails and support requests
  • Send you updates if you’ve subscribed (you can unsubscribe anytime)
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues, abuse, or security problems
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information, and we don’t use your measurements to serve you third-party advertising.

3. Cookies, local storage, and your browser

FitCo currently sets no cookies at all. We use no advertising cookies, no third-party cookies, and no session-recording or session-replay software of any kind.

What we do use is your browser’s local storage, which keeps data on your own device and is never transmitted to us. These are the only keys we write:

  • fitco_v3_answers — your six fitting answers, so your report survives a refresh
  • fitco_fit_result — which fit you matched
  • fitco_quiz_completed — whether you’ve finished the fitting
  • fitco_consent — your analytics choice, so we don’t ask again

You can erase all of it at any time by clearing site data for this site in your browser, which is the equivalent of deleting your FitCo profile. Because it never leaves your device, we hold no copy and have nothing to look up about you.

If analytics is ever enabled, our provider would set one first-party cookie holding a random identifier. We ask before that happens and nothing loads until you agree.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

We honour both. If your browser or an extension sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal or a Do Not Track (DNT) header, we treat it as a standing opt-out: we won’t load analytics, we won’t ask you to, and no identifier is created. You don’t need to do anything else.

4. Analytics

Analytics is currently switched off. No analytics provider receives anything about your visit today.

The only analytics tool FitCo is built to use is PostHog (PostHog, Inc., United States). We do not use Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Vercel Analytics, Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel, Hotjar, or any advertising network, and none of their code is present on this site.

If we turn PostHog on, we will ask for your consent first, and it will be configured to capture only named events we write deliberately — with automatic capture off, session recording off, and surveys off. You can decline, and you can change your mind by clearing site data.

5. Links to retailers

FitCo has no affiliate relationships and earns no commission. The links we publish are ordinary links to the retailer, carrying no referral codes, no tracking parameters and no affiliate tags, and no affiliate network is involved in your visit.

When you follow one, you leave FitCo and the retailer’s own privacy policy takes over. As with any link on the web, your browser tells the retailer which page you came from. We receive nothing back about what you view or buy.

If we ever introduce affiliate links, we will disclose it on the page where the links appear, before you click, and update this policy.

6. Third-party services

As the site is configured today, no third party receives anything about your visit. Fonts are served from our own domain, analytics is switched off, and there are no advertising, chat, embed, or affiliate scripts anywhere on the site.

These are the only providers that can ever be involved, and what each would receive:

  • Our web host — serves the pages, and like any web server processes your IP address to do so.
  • PostHog (analytics) — only if you agree. Would receive the events described in section 1.
  • Our email provider — only if you join the updates list, and only your email address. The list is not open yet; while it is closed no address is collected.

Providers receive only what they need to perform their service and are expected to handle it under their own privacy policies. We will update this list before adding anyone to it.

7. How we share information

We don’t sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • With the service providers described above, so they can perform services for us
  • If required by law, legal process, or a valid government request
  • To protect the rights, safety, or property of FitCo, our users, or others
  • In connection with a business transfer (such as a merger or acquisition), in which case we’ll let users know

8. Data retention

Your fitting answers are kept on your device, not ours, and stay until you clear your browser’s site data. We hold no server-side copy, which means there is nothing for us to retain, search, or hand over.

If you join the updates list, we keep your email address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. If analytics is enabled, event data is retained for the period set in our analytics account.

You can email us to request deletion of anything we hold (see Your rights). The exact retention period for the updates list and for analytics is being finalised and will be stated here before either is switched on.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the following rights:

  • Access — ask what personal information we hold about you
  • Correction — ask us to fix inaccurate information
  • Deletion — ask us to delete information we hold about you
  • Opt out of emails — unsubscribe using the link in any email, or email us
  • Cookie controls — manage cookies through your browser

To exercise any of these, email us at support@fitco.fit. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

10. California privacy rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you’re a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what categories of personal information we collect and how we use it
  • Request access to the specific personal information we’ve collected about you
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate personal information
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising your rights

We do not sell or “share” your personal information as those terms are defined under California law. “Share” in the CPRA sense means disclosing it for cross-context behavioural advertising — we do none of that, we run no advertising, and we use no advertising identifiers. We also do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you.

Because we do not sell or share personal information, there is no “Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information” link to offer. If that ever changes we will add one. As described in section 3, we honour Global Privacy Control regardless.

To make a request, email support@fitco.fit. You may also designate an authorized agent to make a request on your behalf. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

Note that most of what FitCo “knows” about you never reaches us at all — it is held in your own browser. For that data, clearing your browser’s site data is an immediate and complete deletion that does not require asking us.

11. EU / UK visitors (GDPR)

If you’re in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have rights under the GDPR (and UK GDPR), including the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to our processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability.

We process your personal data on these legal bases:

  • Consent — for example, when you subscribe to updates or agree to non-essential cookies (you can withdraw consent anytime)
  • Legitimate interests — to operate, secure, and improve FitCo, in a way that doesn’t override your rights
  • Legal obligation — where we’re required to process data to comply with the law

If our providers process your data outside your country, we rely on appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) where required. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. To exercise your rights, email support@fitco.fit.

12. Children’s privacy

FitCo is intended for users 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us information, email support@fitco.fit and we’ll delete it. If you’re under 18, please use FitCo only with the involvement of a parent or guardian.

13. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including using reputable service providers and standard security practices like encryption in transit (HTTPS). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we can’t guarantee absolute security — but we work to protect your information and to address issues promptly if they arise.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we’ll update the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice. Your continued use of FitCo after an update means you accept the revised policy.

15. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? Email us at support@fitco.fit. FitCo is operated from California, United States.
