How FitCo thinks about fit

A waist size tells only part of the story. The cut changes everything else.

Why pants fit so badly

Most men buy pants by waist size. It doesn't tell you how they fit everywhere else.

Tight in the thighs

Your waist fits but your thighs don't. Common for athletic builds.

Waist gaps

The seat feels right, but the waistband stands away from your back.

Wrong length

Inseam varies by brand, cut, and fabric. Check the garment’s inseam rather than relying on the size label.

Same waist, different fit

Athletic Taper 32 waist
vs
Slim Taper 32 waist

A 32 waist doesn't mean the same thing across brands, cuts, or countries. So you guess, order two sizes, and return one.
